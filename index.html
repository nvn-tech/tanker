How to play
  • ← → walk (limited by the MOVE bar each turn)
  • ↑ ↓ change angle (0–90°)
  • Hold SPACE to charge power, release to shoot. The white ▼ marks your last power.
  • Watch the WIND: it pushes every shot.
  • 1–6 items cost ENERGY and add delay. Rage fills when you take or deal damage: when full, use the Super Shot.
  • Turn order is by delay: walking a lot and using items makes your next turn come later.
  • Knock enemies off the islands, or blast their HP to zero. Drag the screen to look around.
